## 2. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```text
The Khedut is a comprehensive digital agriculture companion designed to help Indian farmers make data-backed farming decisions. Powered by agronomic intelligence, The Khedut brings together real-time mandi prices, instant crop disease diagnosis, digital soil health planning, and hyper-local weather alerts into a single, intuitive multilingual application.

Whether managing small family holdings or multi-acre farms, The Khedut empowers you to optimize input costs, monitor crop cycles, and protect crop yield throughout the agricultural season.

KEY FEATURES:

🌾 AI CROP DISEASE DETECTION
• Scan affected crop leaves using your phone’s camera or gallery.
• Identify common fungal, bacterial, and pest infections across major Indian crops (cotton, wheat, paddy, groundnut, tomato, cumin, and more).
• Review detailed treatment recommendations, preventive cultural practices, and biological management options.
• Download or share professional advisory reports with your local Krishi Kendra or agronomist.

🧪 SOIL ANALYSIS & NPK ADVISORY
• Enter your laboratory soil test values for Nitrogen (N), Phosphorus (P), and Potassium (K), alongside pH and organic carbon.
• Receive customized fertilizer dosage calculations tailored to your selected crop.
• Avoid over-fertilization, balance soil chemistry, and reduce unnecessary input expenditures.

📈 REAL-TIME APMC MANDI PRICES
• Track daily commodity arrival prices across APMC mandis throughout Gujarat, Maharashtra, Rajasthan, and across India.
• Search price trends for grains, pulses, oilseeds, spices, and commercial cash crops.
• Compare minimum, modal, and maximum rates to plan the best time and market to sell your harvest.

🌦️ HYPER-LOCAL WEATHER & FARM ADVISORIES
• Real-time temperature, humidity, wind velocity, and rainfall probability tailored to your village coordinates.
• 7-day agricultural weather outlooks with actionable spraying and irrigation advisories to prevent weather damage.

🗣️ MULTILINGUAL & VOICE-ASSISTED INTERACTION
• Fully localized experience available in English, Gujarati (ગુજરાતી), and Hindi (हिन्दी).
• Integrated voice assistant: speak your questions in your preferred language to search mandi rates or inquire about crop symptoms without complex typing.

📑 PROFESSIONAL PDF ADVISORY REPORTS
• Generate and export comprehensive PDF statements for crop pathology, soil health summaries, and seasonal farming activities.
• Easily share reports via WhatsApp, email, or cloud storage directly from your device.

📍 ACCURATE FARM & BOUNDARY MAPPING
• Map your farm plot coordinates with GPS integration.
• Keep records of active crop cycles, sowing dates, and harvest milestones.

---

IMPORTANT DISCLAIMER:
The Khedut provides agronomic information, diagnostic assistance, and educational tools to aid decision-making. Recommendations are based on agronomic models and user-supplied data. They do not constitute guaranteed yields, insurance coverage, or financial advice. Always verify disease diagnoses with a certified local agronomist or agricultural university specialist before applying chemical treatments.
```
